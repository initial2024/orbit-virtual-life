# Policy system

Orbit Virtual Life has two fictional policy layers.

- National layer: consumption-tax burden, company-tax burden, policy-rate parameter, labor-cost index, business support, consumer protection, energy-cost index and visa-friction parameter.
- Regional layer: transit support, public-safety support, housing-supply support, tourism support and small-business support.

Policy state changes slowly on a simulated quarterly cadence. Macroeconomic and regional signals may change at most a small bounded amount per period. Policy affects company tax/costs/demand, household utilities/transit/housing and neighborhood incident risk.

These values are game calibration. They do **not** claim to reproduce current law, government policy, political positions or the quality of any real jurisdiction. Real legal/tax/immigration decisions must use official sources.
