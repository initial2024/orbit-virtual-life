# Simulation disclaimer

Orbit Virtual Life is a fictional life/economy simulation. Prices, wages, exchange rates, taxes, loans, securities, short selling, lottery mechanics, visas, nationality, public policy, crime/safety, health effects and company outcomes are simplified game models.

Nothing in this repository is financial, investment, legal, tax, immigration, medical, gambling or credit advice. There is no real-money deposit, withdrawal, brokerage, lottery redemption, bank card, government service or visa/nationality decision.
